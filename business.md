# Business brief

## Objective

Make it easy to understand Phyo Thet Paing's enterprise engineering experience, evaluate relevant project work, and start a professional conversation.

## Audiences and visitor journeys

| Audience | Primary need | Website journey |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiters and hiring managers | Assess role fit and career progression | Introduction → experience and skills → résumé download → contact |
| Potential clients | Find relevant ERP or AI experience | Projects → category or keyword search → project details → email |
| Engineering collaborators | Understand technical capabilities and professional presence | Skills → projects → GitHub or LinkedIn |

## Positioning

The portfolio connects Odoo technical leadership with enterprise AI engineering. It emphasizes business understanding, maintainable systems, integrated workflows, and delivery from architecture to production support. Claims and project descriptions are grounded in the supplied résumé.

## Content principles

- Include every project listed in the résumé; keep search and progressive browsing available so the archive remains approachable.
- Describe the owner's role, team size when documented, and actual implementation modules.
- Use CSS illustrations as visual aids, without presenting them as real client-system screenshots.
- Avoid invented business results, performance percentages, client endorsements, or availability claims.
- Maintain consistent contact details and clearly visible GitHub and LinkedIn links.

## Success criteria

Visitors can quickly identify the owner's expertise, find projects relevant to their needs, read the career history, download the résumé, and reach the professional profiles or email address.

Potential future measures include résumé downloads, contact-link clicks, and project engagement. These are proposed measures only: no analytics or measurement service is currently installed.

## Ownership and maintenance

The portfolio owner approves professional content and updates the résumé. Repository maintainers update the site, verify responsive behavior, and keep content consistent with its source. See [skills](skills.md), [requirements](requirement.md), and [deployment](deployment.md).

## Current delivery status

The Django website is implemented and its source is published at [Thar-Paing123/pai-portfolio](https://github.com/Thar-Paing123/pai-portfolio). A public hosting environment has not been configured as part of this project.
